Now

AI symbionts for science communicators

with Miraikan (National Museum of Emerging Science and Innovation) · Sony CSL

What can an AI agent be for an expert? At Miraikan, AI science communicators modeled on real ones talk with visitors, and what they hear goes back to the person they are modeled on, so that both learn. I work on this as a member of the Cybernetic Humanity Studio, Shunichi Kasahara's group at Sony CSL and OIST, within Society with AI Symbionts, and on a follow-up project there.
