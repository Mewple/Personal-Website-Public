FAE-01 delivery robot

A little product design: a robot that delivers fresh air from Earth-born trees to your home on Mars.

Fresh Air Express. How about some natural fresh air, produced by Earth-born trees, delivered to your home on Mars wherever you are?

It supports all-wheel drive for fast delivery when the roads are good, and all-terrain legs for delivering fresh air in the mountains.
